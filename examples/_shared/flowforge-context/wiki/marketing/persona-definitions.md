### Persona 1: "Operations Olivia"

**The Overwhelmed Ops Manager**

![Persona Score: Primary]

#### Demographics
- **Title:** Operations Manager, Head of Operations, RevOps Manager
- **Age Range:** 28-42
- **Company Size:** 20-200 employees
- **Industry:** Tech, SaaS, Professional Services, E-commerce
- **Reports To:** VP Ops, COO, or CEO
- **Team Size:** 2-8 direct reports

#### Psychographics
- **Mindset:** "There has to be a better way"
- **Work Style:** Pragmatic problem-solver, learns by doing
- **Tech Comfort:** Medium-high (uses multiple SaaS tools daily)
- **Decision Style:** Researches thoroughly, starts with free trial

#### Goals
1. Eliminate repetitive manual tasks
2. Reduce errors in data entry and handoffs
3. Free up team time for strategic work
4. Gain visibility into operational bottlenecks
5. Look good to leadership by improving metrics

#### Pain Points
1. "I spend 2 hours every Monday copying data between systems"
2. "When things break, nobody knows until a customer complains"
3. "My team is stuck doing data entry instead of strategic work"
4. "We've tried other tools but they're too complex"
5. "IT won't give me developer resources for automation"

#### Buying Journey
- **Awareness:** Google search for specific pain ("automate HubSpot to Slack")
- **Consideration:** Compares 2-3 tools, reads G2 reviews, watches tutorials
- **Decision:** Free trial → tests with one workflow → expands → converts
- **Typical Timeline:** 2-4 weeks from trial to paid

#### Objections
- "Is this going to break my existing workflows?"
- "Can my non-technical team members use this?"
- "What happens if I leave—will it keep running?"
- "We're a small team, can we really afford another tool?"

#### Preferred Communication
- Email newsletters with templates and tips
- Webinars on specific use cases
- Community forums for peer learning
- Case studies from similar companies

#### What Success Looks Like
- "I automated our lead routing and saved 10 hours/week"
- "We haven't had a data entry error in 3 months"
- "My boss asked how I'm getting so much done"

### Persona 2: "Growth Gary"

**The Scale-Focused Founder/Growth Lead**

![Persona Score: Secondary]

#### Demographics
- **Title:** Founder, Growth Lead, Head of Growth, VP Marketing
- **Age Range:** 26-38
- **Company Size:** 5-50 employees
- **Industry:** SaaS, Tech Startups, Agency
- **Reports To:** CEO or is the CEO
- **Team Size:** 1-5 direct reports

#### Psychographics
- **Mindset:** "Move fast, automate everything"
- **Work Style:** Experiments constantly, data-driven
- **Tech Comfort:** High (builds own Zapier workflows)
- **Decision Style:** Fast, willing to try then switch

#### Goals
1. Scale operations without hiring proportionally
2. Move faster than competitors
3. Build a data-driven growth engine
4. Connect all growth tools seamlessly
5. Impress investors with efficiency metrics

#### Pain Points
1. "I'm hitting Zapier's limits and it's getting expensive"
2. "Our growth stack has 12 tools that don't talk to each other"
3. "We can't afford to hire an ops person yet"
4. "I need real-time alerts when leads come in, not batch syncs"
5. "Every tool wants me to pay enterprise pricing for basic features"

#### Buying Journey
- **Awareness:** Sees competitor comparison, word-of-mouth from founder network
- **Consideration:** Quickly tests 1-2 alternatives to current tool
- **Decision:** If it works better/cheaper, switches fast
- **Typical Timeline:** 1-2 weeks from trial to paid

#### Objections
- "Can this do everything Zapier does?"
- "What's the catch with this pricing?"
- "How do I migrate my existing workflows?"
- "Will this actually scale or will I hit limits again?"

#### Preferred Communication
- Twitter/X and LinkedIn (thought leadership content)
- Founder communities (Slack groups, Discord)
- Comparison content (vs. competitors)
- ROI calculators and pricing transparency

#### What Success Looks Like
- "We 10x'd without hiring another ops person"
- "Our lead response time went from hours to minutes"
- "I stopped paying $400/month to Zapier"

### Persona 3: "Technical Tara"

**The Developer Who'd Rather Not Build This**

![Persona Score: Secondary]

#### Demographics
- **Title:** Full-Stack Developer, Backend Engineer, Solutions Architect
- **Age Range:** 25-40
- **Company Size:** 10-500 employees
- **Industry:** Any (internal tool builder)
- **Reports To:** Engineering Manager, CTO
- **Team Size:** Part of 3-15 person eng team

#### Psychographics
- **Mindset:** "I shouldn't have to build a CRUD app for this"
- **Work Style:** Prefers to focus on core product, not internal tools
- **Tech Comfort:** Very high (could build it themselves)
- **Decision Style:** Evaluates technical architecture, needs API docs

#### Goals
1. Offload internal tooling to off-the-shelf solutions
2. Give non-technical teams self-service automation
3. Reduce "can you build me a quick integration" requests
4. Maintain security and reliability standards
5. Keep architecture simple and maintainable

#### Pain Points
1. "Every team wants a custom integration built"
2. "I've built the same webhook handler 10 times"
3. "Ops keeps asking for developer time for simple automations"
4. "We need something that doesn't require me to babysit it"
5. "Most no-code tools can't handle our edge cases"

#### Buying Journey
- **Awareness:** Recommended by another developer, GitHub/HN
- **Consideration:** Reads API docs, checks error handling, reviews architecture
- **Decision:** Tests edge cases, checks scalability, security review
- **Typical Timeline:** 2-4 weeks (needs to validate technically)

#### Objections
- "Can I write custom code when I need to?"
- "How does error handling work?"
- "What's the retry logic?"
- "Is there a self-hosted option?"
- "How do you handle PII and compliance?"

#### Preferred Communication
- Technical documentation (comprehensive API docs)
- GitHub examples and SDKs
- Changelog and roadmap transparency
- Developer community (Discord, Stack Overflow)

#### What Success Looks Like
- "Ops built their own integration without asking me"
- "It handles failures gracefully, I don't get paged"
- "Our junior devs can use it without help"

### Persona 4: "Enterprise Emma"

**The IT Leader Managing Complexity**

![Persona Score: Tertiary (Enterprise)]

#### Demographics
- **Title:** IT Director, Enterprise Architect, VP IT
- **Age Range:** 38-55
- **Company Size:** 500-5000 employees
- **Industry:** Enterprise (Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing)
- **Reports To:** CIO, CTO
- **Team Size:** 10-50+ in IT org

#### Psychographics
- **Mindset:** "It has to work at scale, securely, reliably"
- **Work Style:** Risk-averse, process-driven, vendor-managed
- **Tech Comfort:** High (but constrained by compliance)
- **Decision Style:** Committee, security review, pilot program

#### Goals
1. Consolidate integration sprawl (too many point solutions)
2. Enable business users without compromising security
3. Reduce shadow IT and ungoverned automation
4. Meet compliance and audit requirements
5. Demonstrate IT as business enabler, not blocker

#### Pain Points
1. "We have 15 different automation tools across departments"
2. "Business teams signed up for tools without telling us"
3. "We need SSO, audit logs, and proper access controls"
4. "Our current enterprise tool costs $200K/year and nobody uses it"
5. "We can't approve tools that don't meet SOC2 requirements"

#### Buying Journey
- **Awareness:** Analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester), peer recommendations
- **Consideration:** RFP process, security questionnaire, pilot program
- **Decision:** Vendor evaluation, negotiation, multi-stakeholder approval
- **Typical Timeline:** 3-6 months minimum

#### Objections
- "Can you fill out our 200-question security questionnaire?"
- "Do you have SOC2 Type II and HIPAA BAA?"
- "What's your SLA and support model?"
- "Can we get a dedicated account manager?"
- "Is there on-prem or VPC deployment?"

#### Preferred Communication
- Account executive relationship
- Executive briefings
- Compliance documentation
- Reference calls with similar enterprises

#### What Success Looks Like
- "We consolidated 8 tools into one platform"
- "Business teams can self-serve within guardrails"
- "Passed our security audit with no findings"

## Anti-Personas (Who We Don't Serve Well)

### "Hobbyist Harry"
- Personal projects, no real business need
- Won't pay, clogs support
- **Filter:** Company email required

### "Complex Carl"
- Needs enterprise iPaaS (MuleSoft, Dell Boomi)
- 1000+ integrations, on-prem systems
- **Filter:** Route to partners or enterprise sales

### "One-Time Wendy"
- Needs a single data migration, not ongoing automation
- **Filter:** Recommend Fivetran, Airbyte, or services partner
