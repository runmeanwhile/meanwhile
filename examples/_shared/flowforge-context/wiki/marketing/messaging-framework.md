**"Automate workflows without writing code."**

"FlowForge is a workflow automation platform that connects your apps and automates repetitive tasks. Unlike complex enterprise tools, FlowForge is designed for operations teams who want to move fast without waiting for developers. You can build your first automation in minutes using our template library, or create custom workflows with our visual builder. Teams typically save 10+ hours per week on manual work."

For **operations teams at growing companies** who **spend too much time on manual tasks**, FlowForge is **a workflow automation platform** that **connects your apps and automates repetitive work**. Unlike **Zapier** (expensive at scale) or **enterprise iPaaS** (too complex), FlowForge is **powerful enough for complex workflows, simple enough for non-technical users, and affordable at any scale**.

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Zapier

| Zapier Says | We Say |
|-------------|--------|
| "Connect your apps" | "Connect your apps AND automate complex workflows" |
| "For everyone" | "For growing teams who've outgrown Zapier" |
| "Simple" | "Simple to start, powerful to scale" |

**Differentiation Hook:**  
"Zapier gets expensive fast. At 10K tasks/month, you're paying $300+. FlowForge gives you more power for less."

**When Zapier Wins:**
- Purely personal automation
- Need 5000+ integrations
- Under 500 tasks/month

## Words to Use / Avoid

### Use These Words
- Automate
- Workflow
- Connect
- Integrate
- Simple
- Powerful
- Fast
- Template
- Visual
- Reliable
- Scalable

### Avoid These Words
- Disrupt / Disruption
- Synergy
- Revolutionary
- Best-in-class
- World-class
- Cutting-edge
- Leverage (as verb)
- Utilize (just say "use")
- Solution (too vague)
- Platform (overused, be specific)

### Technical Jargon Guide

| Jargon | Plain English Alternative |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Webhook | "Real-time notification" |
| API | "Connection" or "integration" |
| Trigger | "Starting event" |
| Action | "Task" or "step" |
| Conditional logic | "Rules" or "if-then" |
| Orchestration | "Coordination" or "workflow" |
| iPaaS | "Automation platform" |
