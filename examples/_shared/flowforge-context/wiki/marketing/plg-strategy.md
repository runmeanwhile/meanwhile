# PLG Strategy & Transition Plan

**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Owner:** Jessica Martinez, VP Marketing  
**Status:** In Progress (Month 6 of 12)

---

## Executive Summary

FlowForge is transitioning from a sales-led growth (SLG) model to a product-led growth (PLG) model. This document outlines our strategy, progress, and learnings.

**Why PLG?**
- Sales-led CAC was $1,847 per customer (unsustainable for SMB)
- Competitors (Zapier, Make) own self-serve market
- Series A investors expect PLG metrics by Q3 2026
- Target market (SMB ops teams) expects to try before buying

**Timeline:** August 2025 - August 2026 (12 months)

## PLG Funnel Defined

```
AWARENESS          ACQUISITION        ACTIVATION         REVENUE           REFERRAL
    |                   |                  |                |                  |
    v                   v                  v                v                  v
[Content/Ads] --> [Free Trial] --> [First Workflow] --> [Paid Plan] --> [Invite Team]
                      |                  |                |                  |
                    2,100/mo          336/mo           231/mo             89/mo
                      |                  |                |                  |
                     100%              16%              11%                4.2%
```

### Funnel Stage Definitions

**Acquisition (Trial Signup)**
- User creates account with email
- No credit card required
- 14-day full-feature trial

**Activation (First Workflow)**
- User creates and runs workflow with real data
- Workflow executes successfully 3+ times
- User demonstrates understanding of value

**Revenue (Conversion)**
- User enters payment method
- Selects paid plan (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise)
- First invoice generated

**Referral**
- User invites teammate who also activates
- Organic word-of-mouth
- G2/Capterra reviews

## Self-Serve vs. Sales-Assisted Segments

### Self-Serve Path (Target: 70% of revenue by 2027)
- **Who:** 1-50 employees, Starter/Pro plans
- **Journey:** Ad/Content → Trial → Self-activate → Convert
- **Touch:** Zero human interaction (support chat only)
- **Target CAC:** <$300
- **Target ACV:** $1,500-$8,000

### Sales-Assisted Path (Target: 30% of revenue by 2027)
- **Who:** 50+ employees, Pro/Enterprise plans
- **Journey:** Trial → PQL score triggers → AE outreach → Demo → Close
- **Touch:** 1-3 sales calls
- **Target CAC:** <$2,500
- **Target ACV:** $15,000-$100,000+

### Product Qualified Lead (PQL) Definition
A trial user becomes a PQL when they meet ANY of these criteria:
1. **Usage-based:** 10+ workflow executions AND invited 2+ teammates
2. **Firmographic:** Company size 50+ AND connected enterprise integration
3. **Intent-based:** Visited pricing page 3+ times AND viewed Enterprise features
4. **Explicit:** Clicked "Contact Sales" or requested demo

**Current PQL Volume:** ~180/month  
**PQL-to-Close Rate:** 24%

## Marketing Strategy Shift

### Before PLG: Lead Generation Focus
- Gated content (ebooks, webinars)
- MQLs passed to SDRs
- Heavy outbound (cold email, LinkedIn)
- Events and sponsorships
- Metric: MQL volume

### After PLG: Activation Focus
- Ungated content (SEO blog, tutorials)
- Product is the lead magnet
- Bottom-of-funnel content (vs. competitors, use cases)
- Community building
- Metric: Activated trial users

## Key Learnings (First 6 Months)

### What's Working
1. **Free trial removes friction** - 10x more top-of-funnel
2. **SEO content drives quality** - Highest activation rate
3. **PQL model works** - Sales closes 24% of PQLs vs. 8% of MQLs before
4. **Self-serve compounds** - Less sales dependency

### What's Not Working
1. **Activation rate too low** - 16% vs. 25%+ benchmark
2. **Time-to-value too long** - 4+ days vs. same-day target
3. **Paid ads inefficient** - Low-intent clicks from display
4. **Support volume spiked** - Not scaled for self-serve yet

### Surprises
1. Enterprise pipeline actually grew (PLG creates awareness)
2. Churn improved (self-serve users stick better than sold users)
3. NPS higher for self-serve (67 vs. 52 for sales-led)

## Q1 2026 PLG Priorities

### 1. Fix Activation (Owned by Product)
- Template-first onboarding
- Reduce time-to-first-workflow to <1 day
- Improve error handling and recovery

### 2. Scale Referral Program (Owned by Marketing)
- Launch formal referral incentives
- "Give $50, Get $50" program
- In-product referral prompts post-activation

### 3. Content Velocity (Owned by Marketing)
- 2 blog posts/week (SEO focused)
- 10 new tutorial videos
- Template marketplace content

### 4. Optimize Paid Channels (Owned by Growth)
- Pause Display advertising
- Double down on Search
- Test TikTok/YouTube for awareness
