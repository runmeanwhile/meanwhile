# Win/Loss Patterns

**Analysis Period:** Q3-Q4 2025  
**Total Deals Analyzed:** 412  
**Win Rate:** 31% (127 won, 285 lost/no decision)

---

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 412 sales opportunities reveals clear patterns in wins and losses. Our strongest performance is with mid-market operations teams evaluating us against Zapier. Our weakest performance is with SMB prospects and enterprise security-focused buyers.

**Key Findings:**
1. Champion seniority is the #1 predictor of win
2. Zapier displacement is our best motion
3. Technical evaluations favor us; procurement evaluations don't
4. Speed of trial success predicts close rate

---

## Win Patterns

### Pattern 1: "Zapier Refugee"
**Win Rate:** 52% (highest)  
**Typical Deal:** $8-15K ACV

**Profile:**
- Currently using Zapier, hitting limitations
- Growing company (50-200 employees)
- Ops or growth leader is champion
- Complex multi-step workflows needed

**Why We Win:**
- Better conditional logic
- More affordable at scale
- Single workflow vs. multiple Zaps

**Trigger Phrases:**
- "We're paying Zapier $X and hitting limits"
- "Our Zaps keep breaking"
- "We need to chain 4 Zaps together to do this one thing"

**Sales Motion:**
1. Acknowledge Zapier's strengths (don't badmouth)
2. Show side-by-side complexity comparison
3. Build their hardest workflow in the demo
4. Offer migration assistance

---

### Pattern 2: "Automation Champion"
**Win Rate:** 47%  
**Typical Deal:** $12-24K ACV

**Profile:**
- Strong internal champion (usually ops)
- Technical enough to evaluate, not technical enough to build
- Has budget authority or strong influence
- Recently joined or promoted (wants quick wins)

**Why We Win:**
- Champion drives evaluation
- Quick time-to-value in trial
- Our product makes them look good

**Trigger Phrases:**
- "I was just hired to fix our operations"
- "We're finally investing in automation"
- "I need to show results by end of quarter"

**Sales Motion:**
1. Enable the champion aggressively
2. Provide assets to help them sell internally
3. Offer quick-win template they can deploy fast
4. Be available for any internal presentation

---

### Pattern 3: "First Automation Tool"
**Win Rate:** 38%  
**Typical Deal:** $5-10K ACV

**Profile:**
- Company new to automation
- Usually SMB/startup (20-75 employees)
- Often founder or ops person evaluating
- Evaluating 2-3 tools simultaneously

**Why We Win:**
- Templates lower barrier to entry
- Visual builder less intimidating than competitors
- Price competitive for entry point

**Sales Motion:**
1. Don't over-demo; keep it simple
2. Focus on templates matching their use case
3. Offer extended trial if needed
4. Fast response time on questions

## Loss Patterns

### Pattern A: "Integration Gatekeeper"
**Win Rate:** 12% (lowest)  
**Typical Deal:** $10-20K ACV

**Profile:**
- Absolute must-have integration we don't support
- Often discovered mid-trial
- No workaround acceptable

**Why We Lose:**
- Missing Monday.com, Pipedrive, ClickUp, etc.
- They literally can't use us without the integration

**Warning Signs:**
- "We use [X] for everything"
- Stack includes tools we don't support
- Early questions about specific integrations

**Counter-Strategy:**
1. Qualify integration requirements early
2. Offer to expedite integration if strategic
3. Know when to disqualify quickly

---

### Pattern B: "Procurement Gauntlet"
**Win Rate:** 18%  
**Typical Deal:** $25K+ ACV

**Profile:**
- Large company with formal procurement
- Security questionnaire, legal review, multiple stakeholders
- 3-6 month evaluation cycles
- Incumbent advantage matters

**Why We Lose:**
- We're unknown (brand risk)
- Zapier/Workato are "safe" choices
- Procurement optimized for incumbent

**Warning Signs:**
- "We need to run this through procurement"
- Multi-stakeholder evaluation committee
- Security questionnaire is 200+ questions
- Evaluation timeline extends repeatedly

**Counter-Strategy:**
1. Get executive sponsor early
2. Have all security docs ready proactively
3. Offer pilot/POC before full procurement
4. Be patient but set clear timelines

---

### Pattern C: "Price Shopper"
**Win Rate:** 15%  
**Typical Deal:** $3-8K ACV

**Profile:**
- Comparing multiple tools primarily on price
- Often small company or tight budget
- Wants free tier or deep discount
- Low engagement during trial

**Why We Lose:**
- Zapier has free tier we don't match
- Make is cheaper at enterprise
- They were never serious buyers

**Warning Signs:**
- First question is about pricing
- Asks for discount before seeing product
- Won't commit time to proper demo
- Minimal trial engagement

**Counter-Strategy:**
1. Qualify budget early
2. Don't discount without commitment
3. Focus on value, not price
4. Know when to walk away

---

### Pattern D: "Technical Overkill"
**Win Rate:** 22%  
**Typical Deal:** $30K+ ACV

**Profile:**
- Engineering-led evaluation
- Needs custom code, self-hosting, or deep customization
- Comparing us to n8n, Temporal, custom build
- Views "no-code" as a limitation

**Why We Lose:**
- They could build this themselves
- Want more control than we offer
- n8n's self-hosted model appeals to them

**Warning Signs:**
- "Can I write Python?"
- "Is there a self-hosted option?"
- "We could just build this ourselves"
- Engineering team doing evaluation

**Counter-Strategy:**
1. Emphasize speed and maintenance burden
2. Show custom code capabilities (JavaScript)
3. If truly technical, may not be our ICP
4. Consider if partnership makes sense

## Ideal Customer Profile (Based on Data)

### Most Likely to Win

**Company:**
- 50-200 employees
- Series A-B funded or $5-20M revenue
- Tech/SaaS or agency vertical
- Growing (hiring, expanding)

**Buyer:**
- Director+ level champion
- Operations, RevOps, or Growth title
- Has budget authority (<$25K)
- Recently hired or promoted

**Situation:**
- Currently using Zapier, hitting limits
- OR new to automation, ready to start
- Clear use case in mind (not exploring)
- Timeline within 30-60 days

**Technical:**
- Stack includes: HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Google Workspace
- No hard requirements for Monday.com, Pipedrive
- No enterprise procurement process
- No on-prem requirements

## Recommendations

### For Sales Team
1. **Qualify champion seniority early** — Below manager, push for higher sponsor
2. **Time-box evaluations** — If not closing in 60 days, re-qualify
3. **Push for Day 1 activation** — Offer live onboarding during trial
4. **Know our weak spots** — Disqualify integration blockers early

### For Product Team
1. **Prioritize Monday.com** — Losing deals to integration gap
2. **Improve trial activation** — 4% win rate if never activated
3. **Enterprise procurement package** — Security docs, references, SLA

### For Marketing Team
1. **Zapier comparison content** — Our best competitive motion
2. **Case studies by industry** — E-commerce and healthcare lagging
3. **Champion enablement** — Assets for internal selling
