# Trademark Policy (Draft)

This document provides draft guidance for using the Meanwhile name and logo.

Status: draft. Confirm legal ownership and update attribution before publishing.

## Permitted Use

- Referential use in documentation, articles, and tutorials.
- Statements that products are "compatible with Meanwhile" or "built using Meanwhile" when accurate.
- Use of the unmodified word mark in truthful, non-confusing ways.

## Prohibited Use

- Using the name or logo to imply endorsement or affiliation without permission.
- Registering domains or social accounts that are confusingly similar to Meanwhile.
- Modifying the logo or creating a composite mark without approval.

## Attribution

Meanwhile is a trademark of its owner. Include an attribution statement in prominent documentation or marketing materials when using the mark.
