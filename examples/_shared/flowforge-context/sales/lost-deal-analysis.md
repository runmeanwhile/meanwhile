# Lost Deal Analysis

**Period:** Q4 2025  
**Total Lost Deals:** 47  
**Total Lost ARR:** $423,000  
**Average Deal Size:** $9,000

---

## Executive Summary

We lost 47 deals worth $423K in potential ARR during Q4 2025. The primary loss reasons were:
1. **Missing integrations** (28%) — $118K lost
2. **Pricing** (23%) — $97K lost
3. **Lost to competitor** (21%) — $89K lost
4. **Technical limitations** (15%) — $63K lost
5. **No decision/timing** (13%) — $56K lost

---

## Loss Reasons Breakdown

### 1. Missing Integrations — 13 deals, $118K

| Missing Integration | Deals Lost | ARR Lost |
|---------------------|------------|----------|
| Monday.com | 4 | $32,000 |
| Pipedrive | 3 | $24,000 |
| ClickUp | 2 | $18,000 |
| Freshdesk | 2 | $22,000 |
| QuickBooks | 2 | $22,000 |

**Typical Quote:**
> "We use [X] for everything. Can't consider a tool that doesn't integrate with it."

**Action Taken:** Monday.com prioritized for Q1 2026. Pipedrive in backlog.

---

### 2. Pricing — 11 deals, $97K

**Breakdown:**
- "Too expensive" (vs. budget): 6 deals
- "Too expensive" (vs. competitor): 3 deals
- "Can't justify at current usage": 2 deals

**Price Sensitivity by Segment:**
| Segment | Deals Lost | Avg Deal Size | Notes |
|---------|------------|---------------|-------|
| Starter prospects | 5 | $2,400 | Wanted free tier |
| Pro prospects | 4 | $9,600 | Compared to Zapier |
| Enterprise prospects | 2 | $36,000 | Compared to Make |

**Typical Quote:**
> "Your product is better, but we can't spend $150/month when Zapier is $99 for our needs."

**Action Taken:** Evaluating pricing model for Q2. Considering usage-based tiers.

---

### 3. Lost to Competitor — 10 deals, $89K

| Competitor | Deals Lost | ARR Lost | Why They Won |
|------------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Zapier | 4 | $28,000 | Brand recognition, integration breadth |
| Make | 4 | $49,000 | Price, specific integrations |
| Workato | 1 | $8,000 | Enterprise features |
| n8n | 1 | $4,000 | Self-hosted preference |

**Competitive Analysis:**

**Zapier Wins:**
- They know the brand, we're unknown
- 5,000+ integrations vs. our 150
- "Good enough" for simpler use cases

**Make Wins:**
- Aggressive enterprise pricing
- More mature e-commerce integrations
- Better data transformation tools

**Typical Quote:**
> "Make offered us 50% less for essentially the same thing." (Prospect comparing apples to oranges, but perception matters)

**Action Taken:** Updated competitive battle cards. Training sales team on Make positioning.

---

## Trial-to-Close Analysis

**Q4 Trial Stats:**
- Total trials started: 6,234
- Reached evaluation stage: 892
- Won: 287
- Lost (tracked): 47
- Lost (untracked/no response): 558

**Lost with Sales Touch vs. Self-Serve:**
| Type | Deals Lost | Conversion Rate |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| Sales-Assisted | 47 | 86% (won 287 of 334) |
| Self-Serve | ~558 | 8% (won ~800 of ~6,500) |

**Insight:** Self-serve conversion is the bigger problem. Most lost deals never talk to sales.

## Recommendations

### Product Priorities
1. **Monday.com integration** — Would have saved 4 deals ($32K)
2. **Amazon real-time inventory** — Would have saved 2 deals ($28K)
3. **Python code blocks** — Would have saved 2 deals ($18K)
4. **Pricing model review** — 11 deals lost to pricing ($97K)

### Sales Process Improvements
1. **Earlier disqualification** — Stop pursing deals where we lack must-have integration
2. **Better trial support** — 15 deals lost during trial, need more proactive help
3. **Competitive training** — Update team on Make's aggressive pricing
4. **Re-engagement program** — Systematically follow up on recoverable deals

### Marketing Adjustments
1. **Integration page updates** — Clearly show what we do/don't support
2. **Pricing transparency** — Calculator on website to set expectations
3. **Comparison content** — More detailed vs. Make content (not just Zapier)
