## How We Prioritize

**Priority Score Formula:**
```
Score = (Request Volume × 0.3) + (Revenue Impact × 0.3) + 
        (Churn Prevention × 0.2) + (Strategic Fit × 0.2)
```

**Revenue Impact:** Does this unlock expansion or new segments?  
**Churn Prevention:** How many churned users mentioned this?  
**Strategic Fit:** Does this support PLG and activation goals?
