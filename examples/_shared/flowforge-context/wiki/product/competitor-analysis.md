**Purpose:** Understand how competitors onboard users and identify opportunities

## Zapier: Best-in-Class PLG Onboarding

### Signup Flow (2 minutes total)
1. Email + password (or Google SSO) - no company info required
2. Single question: "What apps do you want to connect?"
3. Select 2 apps → immediately shown relevant Zaps
4. Click template → pre-populated, just need to auth

### What They Do Well
- **Instant value proposition:** "Connect X to Y" is clear
- **No blank canvas:** Always starts with a template
- **Minimal decisions:** AI suggests relevant templates
- **Progressive disclosure:** Advanced features hidden initially
- **Social proof:** "12,847 people use this Zap"

### What They Do Poorly
- Complex multi-step workflows are hard to visualize
- Pricing becomes confusing at scale
- Limited collaboration features
- No project/folder organization

### Their Activation Metric (Estimated)
~35-40% of trial users create their first Zap within 24 hours

### Key Takeaway
**Zapier optimizes for "first Zap in under 2 minutes."** They sacrifice power for simplicity in onboarding.

## Make (Integromat): Technical Excellence

### Signup Flow (5-8 minutes)
1. Email verification required upfront
2. Choose workspace name
3. Detailed use case survey (6 questions)
4. Template recommendation based on survey
5. Guided scenario creation with inline documentation

### What They Do Well
- **Deep documentation:** Every element has inline help
- **Visual clarity:** Beautiful execution visualization
- **Power users love it:** Full control from day one
- **Use case focus:** Asks "what problem are you solving"

### What They Do Poorly
- Overwhelming for beginners
- Too many options upfront
- No AI assistance
- Longer time to first success

### Their Activation Metric (Estimated)
~20-25% activation, but higher retention among activated users

### Key Takeaway
**Make optimizes for long-term power users, not quick wins.** Works for their technical ICP.

---

## Workato: Enterprise White-Glove

### Signup Flow (Not self-serve)
1. Fill out detailed form (company, size, use case, budget)
2. Sales call scheduled
3. Demo customized to use case
4. Trial only after sales qualification
5. CSM assigned for onboarding

### What They Do Well
- **High-touch = high conversion** (for enterprise)
- **Custom templates** built for each customer
- **Training included** in onboarding
- **Success metrics defined upfront**

### What They Do Poorly
- No PLG motion at all
- Long sales cycle
- Expensive for SMB
- Can't just "try it out"

### Key Takeaway
**Workato proves PLG isn't the only path,** but their TAM is limited to enterprise.

---

## n8n: Developer-First Open Source

### Signup Flow (Cloud version)
1. GitHub or email signup
2. Drops directly into editor (no guidance)
3. Documentation link in corner
4. Community forum for support

### What They Do Well
- **Zero friction for developers:** No hand-holding
- **Full power immediately:** All features available
- **Self-hosted option:** Appeals to privacy-conscious
- **Community templates:** User-generated content

### What They Do Poorly
- Intimidating for non-technical users
- High abandonment rate
- No success-oriented onboarding
- Sink-or-swim mentality

### Their Activation Metric (Estimated)
~10-12% for cloud, but self-hosted unknown

### Key Takeaway
**n8n trades onboarding for flexibility.** Works for developers who don't want guidance.

## Best Practices Synthesis

### What Winners Have in Common
1. **Ask use case early** - Don't make users figure out what to build
2. **Template-first** - Never show a blank canvas
3. **Reduce OAuth friction** - Pre-built, one-click connections
4. **Quick win focus** - Optimize for first 5 minutes, not first feature
5. **Progressive disclosure** - Hide advanced features initially

### FlowForge Gaps

| Best Practice | Zapier | Make | FlowForge | Gap |
|---------------|--------|------|-----------|-----|
| Use case question upfront | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | HIGH |
| Template-first default | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | HIGH |
| One-click integrations | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | MEDIUM |
| Progress indicators | ✅ | ✅ | ⚠️ | LOW |
| AI suggestions | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ | MEDIUM |
| Inline documentation | ⚠️ | ✅ | ❌ | MEDIUM |

## Recommendations

### Immediate (This Quarter)
1. **Copy Zapier's "What apps do you use?" opener** - Most impactful
2. **Make templates the default entry point** - No blank canvas
3. **Add use case survey** - "What do you want to automate?"

### Short-term (Next Quarter)  
4. **AI-powered suggestions** - "Based on your apps, try this"
5. **Simplify OAuth flows** - More pre-built connectors
6. **Add social proof** - "1,234 teams use this workflow"

### Long-term
7. **Interactive tutorials** - Learn by doing, not reading
8. **Personalized onboarding paths** - Different flows for different ICPs
9. **Community templates** - User-generated content library
